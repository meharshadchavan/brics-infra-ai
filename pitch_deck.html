Google AI Integration (25% Weight)
05 / 10

Deep Google AI Execution

Not a wrapper chatbot — Google AI powers data extraction, predictive scoring, and voice parsing.

1. Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (Core Extraction)

Processes raw complaints into deterministic JSON schemas. Evaluates urgency score (1-5), infrastructure domain (Roads, Water, Power, Health), and citizen sentiment.

2. Vertex AI Predictive Scoring Engine

Urban planning optimization model that scores infrastructure demand (1-100) and crafts 1-sentence policy justifications comparing urgency against district budget allocations.

3. Cloud Speech-to-Text & Translation

Native OGG_OPUS speech recognition at 48kHz without ffmpeg transcoding, paired with Google Cloud Translation API across 7 languages.

Directly fulfills Hack2Skill mandatory Google AI requirement with three coordinated GCP AI services
